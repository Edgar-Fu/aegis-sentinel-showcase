Aegis Sentinel

A governed-vision showcase that refuses unsafe automation, separates conflicting evidence from low confidence, persists canonical decision evidence, and verifies that a stored decision can be replayed without hiding tampering.

Scope: this repository executes a deliberately simplified public policy and controlled synthetic-fixture adapter. The full private implementation’s real-model integration, calibration corpus, complete policy library, advanced reliability modules, and full validation harness are not included.

What this demo proves

  • Fail closed: bad image evidence stops before detection.
  • Conflict aware: independent-source disagreement escalates.
  • Auditable: ordered rules explain every route.
  • Replayable: evidence is sealed with SHA-256 and re-evaluated.
  • Tamper evident: a one-byte change blocks replay.
